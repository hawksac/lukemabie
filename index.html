Five rules. One truck. Nothing broken.

I'm Luke Mabie, a data and software engineer in Omaha who likes problems you can describe instead of program. The truck in this demo packs itself from five rules that say what a good load looks like, not how to load it. That's the idea behind most of what I build.

A logic program, packing a moving truck

packed 0/15 · broken 0 ready 
% every item gets exactly one spot
1 { at(I,X,Y) : spot(I,X,Y) } 1 :- item(I).% one thing per square
:- fills(I,X,Y), fills(J,X,Y), I < J.% nothing floats in midair
:- at(I,X,Y), Y > 0, not held_up(I).% heavy things ride on the floor
:- at(I,_,Y), heavy(I), Y > 0.% nothing goes on top of fragile stuff
:- on(_,F), fragile(F).
